Dear Shahnaz,

I owe you a sincere apology. When I told you that I was getting married somewhere else, I hurt you. I know those words must have been painful and confusing, especially coming from someone with whom you had shared a bond.

I am not going to make excuses or pretend that a simple “sorry” can undo what happened. I should have been more honest and more careful with your feelings. You did not deserve to be left carrying hurt because of the way I handled things.

Even though we still talk, I understand that talking does not automatically make everything okay. I don't want to act as if nothing happened or expect you to move on just because I have finally found the words to apologise.

I am truly sorry for hurting you, Shahnaz. Whatever you choose to feel or do after reading this, I will respect it. You do not owe me forgiveness, a reply, or another chance.

With sincerity,
Someone who should have handled your heart with more care.

No pressure. Just honesty.

I don't expect one message or one page to repair everything. If you ever want to tell me how this affected you, I will listen without arguing, defending myself, or asking you to hide your feelings.

“I can't rewrite what happened. I can take responsibility for it, and be more honest from here.”
